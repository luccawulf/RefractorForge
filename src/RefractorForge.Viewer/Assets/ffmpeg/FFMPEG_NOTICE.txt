RefractorForge bundles FFmpeg to decode Bink (.bik) videos for in-editor playback.

FFmpeg is a separate work, the property of its authors, and is NOT part of
RefractorForge. It is used here only as an external command-line program
(ffmpeg.exe is launched as a child process; RefractorForge does not link against
the FFmpeg libraries).

This build links libx264 and is therefore distributed under the terms of the
GNU General Public License, version 2 or later (GPL v2+). The FFmpeg / libx264
source code is available from:

    https://ffmpeg.org/          (FFmpeg)
    https://www.videolan.org/developers/x264.html   (x264)

FFmpeg is a trademark of Fabrice Bellard, originator of the FFmpeg project.

Files in this folder (ffmpeg.exe + its required runtime DLLs) are an unmodified
redistribution of an FFmpeg build. To use a different FFmpeg, replace ffmpeg.exe
(and its DLLs) here, or drop an ffmpeg.exe on your PATH.
